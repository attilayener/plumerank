**1.1538 falls in the middle band. The project proceeds.**

## 2. What that means, stated carefully

On 2026-09-05 the binding quantity was **1.5385 — above the abort cutline.** `gonogo §7.3`
declined to fire the abort because the instrument was wrong: `constants.py` carried a-priori
fusion weights while `build-plan.md` D11 specifies "a weighted logistic, weights fit on DEV".
`ruling_d11_contribute.md` §3 then fixed that refusal in advance, and §4 fixed what would bind
instead — **the next measurement of fog's own rate on a fitted instrument, whichever way it
cut.**

It cut favourably. That is worth saying plainly *because* it is favourable: the ruling was
written when the number could as easily have come back at 1.8, and it committed to escalating a
descope to the human gate if it had. **Fitting the weights is what moved this project off its
abort branch** — a step that had been outstanding since the plan's own D11 and was not
discovered by the plan.

## 4. The finding that survived fitting, and it is now the project's real problem

`gonogo §7.4` reported that **cloud shadow is a worse adversary than fog** and offered three
readings. Fitting settles two of them.

**Reading 2 is falsified.** §7.4 proposed that "the unfitted fusion weights over-weight S3
relative to S1 and S2, so a hard-edged moving region scores high on anchoring and anisotropy
without S3 being able to pull it down." The fit says the opposite: DEV supports **more** weight
on S3 (2.6 → 4.0931) and far **less** on S1 (1.9 → 0.6164) and S2 (1.4 → 0.3856). The a-priori
weights *under*-weighted the fog discriminator and *over*-weighted anchoring and anisotropy.
Cloud shadow's dominance is not an S3 weighting artefact.

**Reading 1 is now the live one, and it has company.** Cloud shadow still takes **3.86
alerts/camera-night on 21% of the windows**, and `low_cloud` — which the unfitted instrument
rated harmless at 1.25 — **more than tripled to 4.25**, overtaking it as the worst adversary
in the arm.

> **The two worst adversaries are now low cloud (4.25) and cloud shadow (3.86). Fog, the
> adversary this project was designed around and named its risk register after, is third at
> 1.15 — and both cloud classes cost more than three times what it does.**
>
> `project.json` records the biggest risk as "Fog. A coastal marine layer both anchors to
> terrain and grows vertically… if diffuse-boundary contrast does not separate smoke's soft
> edge from a fog bank's, fog consumes the entire budget every morning." On the fitted
> instrument fog consumes **half** its allowance while two cloud classes consume double
> theirs. The predicted risk is not the realised one.

This belongs in `REPORT.md` §limitations whatever else happens, as §7.4 already committed.

## 5. Three things this verdict does NOT license

1. **It is not a GO on the premise.** The Sep 1 separability gate has still never run —
   `spike/boxes.json` does not exist and §6 of `gonogo_2026-09-01.md` is still `_pending_`.
   D11 was designed as the second half of a two-part gate; the first half is still owed. See
   `s3_gate_mechanical_attempt.md`.
2. **The fog class may not be fog.** `fog_class_review.md` finds that of 54 of the 60 frames
   inspected, roughly half show no marine layer at all — and §7.5 records that the
   `fog_marine` predicate was re-derived from that same set. **So 1.1538 is a rate on a class
   whose membership is in doubt.** A comfortable number on the wrong class is not
   reassurance, and this caveat travels with the verdict wherever it is quoted.
3. **`low_cloud` at 4 windows and `cloud_shadow` at 7 are small.** §7.4's third reading — that
   these classes are not representative — is untouched by anything here and remains open.

## 6. Provenance

- Weights: `results/fusion_fit_2026-09-06.json` — L2 logistic (IRLS), balanced class weights,
  bias unpenalised, λ=1.0, 2291 frames over 29 DEV sequences, **leave-one-sequence-out AUC
  0.6387** (in-sample 0.6566).
- Labels: the ignition clock. A frame is positive from `t0_index` onward, negative before,
  every frame of a non-fire window negative. No margin and no smoothing — a band would be a
  fitted parameter hidden in the labelling step.
- Split: `scripts/class_split.py`, threshold solved on the **whole** arm and never per class.
- DEV and CONFUSE were both replayed at the fitted constants; `results/hold_access.log`
  records no HOLD read, because none happened.
