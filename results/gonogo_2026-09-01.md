# S3 separability gate — GO / NO-GO

**Date:** 2026-09-01 (D3) · **Days to submission deadline:** 55
**Question:** does diffuse-boundary contrast (S3) separate smoke's soft edge from a fog bank's?

> **This document was written BEFORE the script was run.** Sections 1–5 are fixed commitments.
> Section 6 is filled in from the output, whatever it says. That order is what makes this a gate
> rather than a formality — see `specs/complexity.md` §6.5.

---

## 1. What is being decided

Whether PlumeRank's anchored plume-growth primitive has a viable third statistic. S1 (base anchoring)
and S2 (vertical growth anisotropy) are both satisfied by a coastal marine layer: fog anchors to
terrain and grows vertically. **S3 is the only statistic that discriminates.** If it cannot, the
primitive fails against its real adversary and the project premise is gone.

This gate does **not** test stabilisation, MOG2, tracking, hazard fusion or the budget solver. A rate
per camera-night is not computable from static crops; that question is the **D11 budget checkpoint**
(2026-09-09), not this one.

## 2. The decision rule — fixed in advance

95% bootstrap CI on AUC (2,000 draws, seed 20260901, resampling each class independently):

| Verdict | Rule | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **NO-GO** | CI **upper** bound < 0.70 | **Abandon PlumeRank. Switch to the fallback project, with 55 days left.** |
| **GO** | CI **lower** bound ≥ 0.70 | Proceed. D11 becomes confirmation, not rescue. |
| **CONDITIONAL GO** | CI **straddles** 0.70 | Proceed, **and D11 becomes a hard abort gate** whose criteria are §4 below. |

**The NO-GO sentence, written before the number exists:**

> If the 95% CI upper bound on AUC is below 0.70, S3 cannot separate smoke from fog, the anchored
> plume-growth primitive has no discriminating statistic, and PlumeRank is abandoned on 2026-09-01 in
> favour of the fallback project. This is a good outcome today and a catastrophic one on 10 October. It will
> not be renegotiated after seeing the number, and no variant search will be used to overturn it —
> the variants are enumerated in advance (§3) and their selection cost is disclosed (§5).

The decision is taken **on the interval, not the point estimate.** At n=60/60 the standard error on
AUC is ≈0.045, so the 95% CI half-width is ≈0.087. A point-estimate rule at these thresholds would
return a confident verdict on noise roughly 27% of the time when the true AUC is 0.70.

## 3. The variants, enumerated before the run

Each is a flag on the same script, not a new code path. Run only if v0's CI straddles the cutline.

| | Variant | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| v0 | raw-frame ratio | the specified statistic |
| v1 | multi-scale Laplacian, max over three σ | plume boundaries live at a different scale than fog banks |
| v2b | halved boundary band width | the band width is a guess |
| v3 | log of the ratio | ratios of energies are heavy-tailed |
| v4 | normalised by ROI mean intensity | S3 should not be measuring absolute brightness |
| **v5** | **S3 on the temporal difference frame** | **the most likely rescue** — a fog bank's boundary is near-stationary between frames while a plume's advances, so differencing suppresses fog's edge and preserves smoke's |

## 4. The D11 abort criteria — written now, measured 2026-09-09

At the threshold `rank.py` calibrates to 2.0 alerts/camera-night over the whole CONFUSE arm, measure
what the **fog windows alone** contribute:

- **< 0.5 alerts/camera-night from fog** → clear. The original GO condition is met on real evidence.
- **> 1.5 from fog** → fog eats the budget every morning. **The primitive has failed against the real
  adversary; the descope decision is taken on D11 with 47 days left.**
- **between** → publish the number, proceed, and it becomes a named limitation in `REPORT.md` and a
  row on `/results`.

## 5. The instrument, and its known limitations

- `spike/s3_separation.py` (~200 lines), `spike/urls.txt` (120 URLs), `spike/boxes.json`
  (120 hand-drawn boxes, one per image). Committed, so the gate is reproducible by someone else.
  `spike/frames/` is gitignored — HPWREN imagery, regenerable from `urls.txt`.
- **Smoke: 60 frames from 20 FIgLib sequences** (2024–2026), at t+15/+25/+35 min after the recorded
  visible-plume appearance. The 20 sequences were **screened for an actually-visible plume** — about
  half of FIgLib's sequences name a fire that is not in that camera's frame, and an unscreened sample
  would have measured haze. Screening was a localized-change score between t−30 and t+30 min,
  followed by visual confirmation of every retained sequence.
- **Fog: 60 frames from ~20 dawn mornings** at 06:00–08:59 Pacific, drawn from the HPWREN
  medium-term camera archive across June–August 2026, on the same camera platforms
  (`hp-*`, `wilson-*`, `tdlln-*`). Selected by a within-camera fog score, then visually confirmed as
  genuine marine layer. One total-whiteout morning was excluded: with the camera inside the cloud
  there is no boundary to box, and this statistic measures boundaries.
- **LIMITATION, stated up front:** the smoke frames are FIgLib (2024–2026) and the fog frames are the
  medium-term archive (2026). Same camera platforms, **different dates and partly different years.**
  The spec assumed dawn fog could be drawn from FIgLib itself; it cannot — FIgLib publishes only
  ±40 min around each ignition, and ignitions cluster between 11:00 and 17:00 Pacific, so dawn is
  almost absent from it. This is a real difference between the two classes and it is disclosed here
  rather than discovered later.
- **LIMITATION:** boxes are hand-drawn by one person who knows which class each image belongs to.
  The box placement is therefore not blind. Mitigation: the band width `b` is fixed a priori and no
  constant is fitted anywhere in the statistic.

## 7. D11 budget checkpoint — measured 2026-09-05, four days late

> Section 4 wrote these criteria on 2026-09-01, before any of this was known. This section
> records what the instrument returned. **It does not decide anything**, for the reason in
> 7.3: two of the preconditions the criteria assume are not met, and a gate evaluated
> outside its own preconditions is a number wearing the authority of a decision.

### 7.1 What was run

`scripts/bench.py --arm confuse` over `data/manifest_confuse.json`, then the per-class
split recomputed with `plumerank.rank.realised_rate` at the threshold the whole arm
calibrated. Both are committed tooling; nothing here came from a bridge or a scratch script.

| | |
|---|---|
| Arm | **33 windows**, not the 45 the spec asked for — see 7.4 |
| Threshold, solved on the whole arm | **0.345703** |
| Whole-arm realised rate | **2.0000** alerts/camera-night (target 2.00, uncapped) |
| Escalation | 2.13% of frames — above the 2.00% design target, as it must be on a 33-camera roster where `rank <= 2` ceilings it at 6.06% |
| Model calls | PlumeRank **2** / camera-night · B1 **3,645** / camera-night |

### 7.2 The per-class split, which is what §4 asked for

| class | windows | alerts | own rate | share of the arm's 2.00 budget |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **fog_marine** | 13 | 20 | **1.5385** | **0.6061** |
| cloud_shadow | 7 | 31 | 4.4286 | 0.9394 |
| exposure_hunting | 5 | 10 | 2.0000 | 0.3030 |
| low_cloud | 4 | 5 | 1.2500 | 0.1515 |
| lens_artefact | 3 | 0 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |
| dust | 1 | 0 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |

**§4's criteria do not resolve on these two numbers, and the ambiguity is in §4, not in the
data.** It says *"measure what the fog windows alone contribute"* against cutlines of 0.5
and 1.5, and there are two honest readings of "contribute":

- **fog's share of the arm's budget = 0.6061** → *between* → publish it, proceed, name it a
  limitation in `REPORT.md`.
- **fog's own rate, on fog windows alone = 1.5385** → *above 1.5* → **abort, descope to
  the fallback project.**

The first reading is the one the sentence most naturally supports — a contribution to a
budget is a share of that budget — and it is also the favourable one. **That is exactly why
this document does not adopt it.** §2's discipline was that the rule would not be
renegotiated after seeing the number, and picking the favourable reading of an ambiguous
rule after the fact is renegotiation wearing a different hat.

### 7.3 Two preconditions are not met, and both push the same way

1. **The Sep 1 separability gate has still never run.** `spike/boxes.json` does not exist,
   so §6 is `_pending_`. D11 was designed as the *second half* of a two-part gate whose
   first half returns CONDITIONAL GO or better; with no verdict at all, D11 has no defined
   status to inherit.
2. **The fusion weights are not fitted.** the build plan D11 specifies `rank.py` with "a
   weighted logistic, weights fit on DEV". `plumerank/constants.py` still carries the
   a-priori values written before any data was seen. So this is a D11-*shaped* measurement
   taken with unfitted constants, and the fitting it is missing is the step most likely to
   move it.

**The honest status is therefore: measured, recorded, undecided.** The number is published
here whichever way it eventually cuts, which was the commitment.

### 7.4 The finding nobody predicted, and it is the important one

**Cloud shadow is a worse adversary than fog.** It takes **47% of the arm's alert budget on
21% of its windows** — an own rate of 4.43 against fog's 1.54 — and it is the class
`seed-data.md` §4 predicted S3 "should reject most cleanly", because a cloud shadow's
boundary is *hard* and that is precisely what S3 measures.

The project's entire premise is that fog is THE adversary and S3 is the statistic that
rejects it. On this arm S3 is doing better against fog than against the class it was
supposed to find easy. Three readings, none yet distinguished:

- S3 is working as designed and cloud shadow is simply harder than anyone thought;
- the unfitted fusion weights over-weight S3 relative to S1 and S2, so a hard-edged moving
  region scores high on anchoring and anisotropy without S3 being able to pull it down;
- the 7 cloud_shadow windows are not representative — it is 7 windows.

**This is a real result and it belongs in `REPORT.md` §limitations whatever the resolution.**
It is also the strongest argument in the corpus for fitting the weights on DEV before
anything is published: the measurement that embarrasses the premise is the one worth making.

### 7.5 Arm composition — 33 windows, and it must never be quoted as 45

The predicate that selects fog was re-derived from the 60 hand-labelled fog frames in
`spike/`, because the original cutlines admitted **0 of 60** of them (`edge_energy` 12.6–60.1
against a `<= 18.0` gate; `sky_ground_ratio` 1.46–11.27 against `<= 1.05`, with the sign
inverted). The replacements are the **medians of the labelled class** — `deck_fraction`
0.12847 (AUC 0.706, 95% CI [0.609, 0.798]) and `sky_greyness` -0.13140 (AUC 0.674) — which
is a rule that cannot be walked until a quota falls out, and it did not: it yielded 13 fog
windows against a quota of 18.

`fog_marine` 13/18 and `dust` 1/8 are **supply limits**, verified as the arithmetic maximum
over every assignment: fog is capped by its 13 distinct qualifying cameras and dust by its 1.
No neighbouring class was relabelled to cover the gap. **Nothing in this arm may be published
as 45**, and no document may quote `class_quotas`.
