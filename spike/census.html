Three answers, and the middle one is the point

Marine layer
A fog bank with a visible boundary: an undercast sea of cloud lying below the ridge line, a wall of fog with a top edge in shot, or the camera itself immersed in dense fog. S3 measures a boundary — this is the only category that gives it one to measure.
Overcast ceiling
A grey stratus deck filling the upper frame with no boundary visible in the shot. Meteorologically this may well be the same marine layer, closed over the camera. It is kept separate because the disagreement is the finding — collapsing it into either neighbour hides it.
Neither
Clear or lightly-clouded dawn over hills. The dominant feature is the rising sun, a sky gradient, cirrus or contrails. Haze without a deck is neither.

If you cannot decide between two, take the weaker claim: overcast rather than marine layer, neither rather than overcast. An under-called class is recoverable; an over-called one is what put the project here.
